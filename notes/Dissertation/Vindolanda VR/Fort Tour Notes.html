Fort Tour Notes

Very rough notes taken during tour of the fort.

Early forts were wooden. Would be demolished when garrison recalled. Covered in clay, preserves wood

Wax tablets reused. Don’t often survive. Wooden tablets and ink recovered. Slow to decipher

Altar to Vulcan discovered 1913. Means white field. Settled for water supply with altar to God of water

Turrets 10-13m high. Wall took 8 years to build

Aqueducts were present

Fort wall 5m high. Same as Hadrian. Wooden for Hadrian time 2 guard towers size of turret

Bath house as social centre with weighted dice. Curved roof to control water flow. Heated rooms. Warm 40c. Hot 60c. Furnace room to channel air. Probably wood fired. Tile floori. Used wooden clogs. Covered in oil. Soldiers often sick due to poor hygiene here

Many penises as graffiti. Nothing’s changed here.

Many types of dogs. Taxes based on street frontage. Butcher shop that may have cooked food for soldiers headed to bathhouse

Pub had broken glass imported from cologne

Metal working near pub. Possibly counterfeit coins.

Tablets found at bonfire site. Netherlands garrison supposed to burn correspondence. Extinguished by rain. Correspondence survived.

Birthday invitation from wife of commanding officer. 11th September. Claudia   slave scribe. High status women were lonely

Marks in stone from sharpening swords

Granaries had ventalafes floors. Kept years supply of grain. Dogs used to catch vermin. Some cat bones found. Present but not populous

Garrisons with wooden upper floors and communal ovens.

8 per conterbernia room. Larger room for centurion of 10 centerburnia. 12-14 year old girl found. Likely a slave. Body hidden

Head quarters directly opposite north gate. Most forts followed same pattern. 500 soldiers lived in fort and congregate at headquarters for orders pay feasts or discipline. 500=1 cohort of auxiliaries recruited from conquered areas. Paid less. Legionaries built wall, auxiliaries provided labour. Standard guarded 24 hours. Bank was here. Tribune for senior centurion and effigy of current emperor life sized.

Ventilated room as weapon store.

Later forts contain Christian artifacts starting 4th century
