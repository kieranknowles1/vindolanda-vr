Locomotion

Smooth locomotion can cause motion sickness in VR, especially for new users. Games often work around this with teleport movement (less immersive, but should have as an option) or by adding vignette while moving.

Options

  • Teleport (SteamVR Home)
    • Good for beginners
    • Less immersive
  • Smooth (Blade and Sorcery)
    • Can cause sickness (reduced with vignette)
    • More immersive
    • Forward can be defined as controller forward or look forward
      • Personally prefer controller forward, but should have an option
      • Blade and Sorcery seems to use a smoothed controller direction, H3VR doesn't which causes nausea when reaching behind back.
  • Turning - snap or smooth
    • Keep both as options, I personally don't like either and prefer physical turning.
